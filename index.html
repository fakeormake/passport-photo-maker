Free 2×2 Inch Passport Photo Maker

The 2×2 inch photo is a common format used for US passport and visa applications. This tool crops your photo to 600 × 600 pixels, calculated at a 300 DPI target, so the physical size works out to exactly 2×2 inches when printed at that resolution. Correct dimensions are only one part of a compliant photo — see the requirements section below before submitting.

35×45mm Passport Photo Maker

35×45mm is a commonly used photo size for UK, EU and many other international passport, visa and ID applications. At a 300 DPI target this works out to approximately 413 × 531 pixels. Individual passport and visa authorities can have additional requirements beyond size, so always check current guidance for your specific application.

Does This Website Upload My Photo?

No. This tool is built to process your photo entirely inside your browser, using your device's own memory and processing power. Your image file is read locally, edited on an in-browser canvas, and the finished file is generated for download without ever being sent to a server operated by this website.

Passport Photo Requirements

Photo requirements can include, depending on the country and document type:

  • Dimensions
  • Head size and position
  • Eye position
  • Facial expression
  • Background
  • Lighting and shadows
  • Clothing
  • Overall image quality

This tool helps you hit the correct dimensions and position your photo using visual guides, but it does not verify compliance. Always confirm the current requirements with the relevant passport, visa or ID authority before submitting a photo.
